--- UEFA Champions League Trivia & Records ---

1. Most Successful Clubs
Real Madrid holds the record for the most UEFA Champions League (formerly European Cup) titles, having won the competition 15 times as of 2024. AC Milan is the second most successful club with 7 titles, followed by Bayern Munich and Liverpool with 6 titles each.

2. All-Time Top Scorers
Cristiano Ronaldo is the all-time leading goalscorer in the history of the Champions League, with 140 goals. Lionel Messi is second with 129 goals. Robert Lewandowski ranks third. Cristiano Ronaldo also holds the record for the most goals in a single Champions League season, scoring 17 goals in the 2013-2014 campaign.

3. Fastest Goal
The fastest goal in Champions League history was scored by Roy Makaay for Bayern Munich against Real Madrid on March 7, 2007. He found the back of the net in just 10.12 seconds after kickoff. 

4. Historic Finals
One of the most dramatic finals occurred in 1999 when Manchester United defeated Bayern Munich 2-1. Bayern led for most of the match, but United scored two goals in stoppage time (by Teddy Sheringham and Ole Gunnar Solskjær) to win the trophy. Another famous final is the 2005 "Miracle of Istanbul," where Liverpool came back from a 3-0 halftime deficit against AC Milan to tie the game 3-3 and eventually win on penalties.

5. Player Appearances
Cristiano Ronaldo holds the record for the most appearances in the UEFA Champions League, having played in 183 matches during his time with Manchester United, Real Madrid, and Juventus. Iker Casillas is second with 177 appearances.
